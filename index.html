Engineering the tools that plan the network.

I'm a Network Planning & Design Engineer who bridges the gap between raw infrastructure data and the people who need to act on it. My background in Network Operations gave me a deep understanding of how networks fail — now in NPD, I use that to design systems that don't.

Alongside my core engineering role, I independently build internal tooling — Streamlit dashboards, SSH automation platforms, geospatial analysis apps, and KPI reporting systems. I also manage the server-side infrastructure these tools run on, including Proxmox virtualisation environments and VM logical networking.

I believe the best engineers obsess over the experience of the people using their work. Every tool I build prioritises clarity, reliability, and speed.
